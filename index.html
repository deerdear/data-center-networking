Vocabulary of the fabric

XPU = any accelerator (GPU / TPU / NPU). Sizes, counts, latencies and bandwidths are representative of current-generation (2024–25) systems and vary by vendor and configuration — read them as orders of magnitude, not datasheet values. Power figures are rough facility estimates (≈120 kW per 72-GPU rack incl. cooling & networking): a hall ≈ 65 MW, a campus ≈ 300 MW, a full region ≈ 2 GW — the last comparable to a mid-size city or a large power plant.
